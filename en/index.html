01 / About

I start by understanding the problem. The technology comes second.

What I do

I help organise manual or loosely defined processes and build AI solutions that can be tested on real cases and integrated into day-to-day work.

I am a computer engineer, MSc graduate and PhD candidate, with experience in research and teaching at the University of Coimbra. Research taught me to ask better questions, evaluate results carefully and be clear about limitations. I bring the same habits to product development.

Since 2025, I have also worked in consulting, solution development and AI training. I especially enjoy projects where I can learn the process from the people who know it best and turn that knowledge into something concrete.
